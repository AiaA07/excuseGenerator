==============================================================
SITE: Excuse Generator 3000
==============================================================

ROLE / CONTEXT
--------------
Role: You are Chief Creative Director for Pixar 3D Movies.
Context: You are making 
the film "King Arthur and The Knights of Barkalot." Barkalot is a "doggie Camelot" and all the characters of Camelot are Dogs. The film features a "Doggie Cafe" called Château Barkalot. You, the Chief Creative Director, have commissioned a highly talented Web Designer, Jeremy, to create the **Excuse Generator 3000** website — a humorously anachronistic tool where visitors generate medieval-flavored excuses for modern life, rated by a "Knightly Lie Detector."

Assign each knight a different dog breed of your choice. Render every character in clean Pixar 3D cartoon style — soft subsurface scattering, painterly lighting, expressive oversized eyes, stylized fur shader.

Characters (for "Knights of Alibi" section):
- King Arthur – the leader (Golden Retriever)
- Sir Lancelot – greatest warrior (Doberman Pinscher)
- Sir Galahad – pure knight (Cavalier King Charles Spaniel)
- Sir Gawain – loyal and noble (German Shepherd)
- Sir Percival – Grail seeker (Irish Wolfhound)
- Sir Tristan – tragic romance (Bloodhound)
- Sir Bedivere – returns Excalibur (Old English Sheepdog)
- Sir Kay – Arthur's foster brother (Pug)
- Sir Bors – one of the Grail achievers (Boxer)
- Sir Mordred – traitor (Siberian Husky)
- Sir Gareth – humble, noble knight (Beagle)
- Sir Agravain – exposes Lancelot/Guinevere (Rottweiler)
- Sir Gaheris – Gawain's brother (Border Collie)
- Sir Palamedes – Saracen knight (Saluki)
- Sir Ector – Arthur's foster father (Bulldog)
- Sir Lamorak – one of the strongest knights (Great Dane)
- Merlin – The Wizard (Dachshund — unexpectedly magical)
- Lady Guinevere (Cocker Spaniel)

BUILD STACK
-----------
1. Stitch (layout generation)
2. UI UX Pro Max (style / palette / typography selection)
3. Firecrawl (reference scrape)
4. Magic / 21st.dev (marketing blocks)
5. Nano Banana 2 (hero + feature imagery)
6. Brand skill (voice + messaging)
7. Design skill (logo, icons, OG image)
8. Design System skill (token architecture)
9. UI Styling skill (shadcn/ui interactive components)

PURPOSE
-------
A comedic excuse generator tool that helps users craft medieval-themed excuses for modern problems (work, dating, sports, general chaos), then rates their believability on an animated "Knightly Lie Detector" meter. Visitors can browse excuses by category, generate random ones, submit custom excuses, and share the funniest results.

==============================================================
STEP 2 — BRAND VOICE (for `brand` skill)
==============================================================

Voice archetype: The Jester-Sage — Arthurian whimsy meets modern internet humor. Speaks like a court jester who secretly runs a popular meme account.

Tone dimensions (scale 1–5):
- Formal ↔ Casual:        1 (very casual — this is a joke generator)
- Serious ↔ Playful:      5 (extremely playful)
- Reserved ↔ Expressive:  5 (very expressive)
- Modern ↔ Timeless:      2 (modern with medieval costume)

Voice rules:
- Sprinkle medieval vocabulary sparingly: "hark," "verily," "I beseech thee," "thou hast been caught." Max 2 medieval phrases per excuse.
- Dog puns are earned, not stacked. Max one pun per excuse.
- Second person ("you," "thy") — direct and funny.
- Short, punchy sentences. The lie detector's verdict should feel like a playful roast from a friend.

Words to use: hark, verily, beseech, thou, thy, noble, quest, dragon, squire, tankard, alas, farewell, EXCOMMUNICADO (for very bad lies)
Words to avoid: leverage, synergy, solutions, premium, optimal, deliverables

Messaging framework:
- Tagline (≤7 words):        "Hark! A lie of noble proportion."
- Value prop (≤20 words):    "Generate medieval excuses for modern problems. Our Knightly Lie Detector rates your alibi's believability."
- Key phrases (3–5):         "The dragon ate my quest," "I was jousting," "My squire overslept," "Verily, that's a lie," "Pull up a tankard of truth"

==============================================================
STEP 3 — DESIGN SYSTEM (for `ui-ux-pro-max` + `design-system`)
==============================================================

Style keywords: retro, camp, funny, warm, playful, cozy, modern, friendly, inviting, clean, Pixar 3D, soft rounded, storybook-illustrative

Preferred style direction: soft claymorphism with painterly storybook accents — generous corner radii, tactile shadows, no sharp geometric brutalism. Hero section may break grid with layered illustration. The lie detector meter should look like a whimsical dashboard from a cartoon castle.

COLOR SYSTEM (semantic roles — let ui-ux-pro-max pick the exact 161-palette entries that match these roles):
- Primary:         warm sunset red/coral (castle banners, lie detector "LIE" zone)
- Secondary:       deep royal purple (twilight sky, generator panel background)
- Tertiary:        forest green (meadow / "TRUTH" zone on meter)
- Accent:          warm golden honey (candlelight, meter needle, glowing LEDs)
- Surface-base:    cream / parchment off-white
- Surface-raised:  soft warm beige
- Ink:             deep espresso brown (NOT pure black)
- Success/info:    sage green / sky blue from the palette

Contrast requirement: WCAG AA minimum, AAA preferred for body copy.

TYPOGRAPHY (let ui-ux-pro-max pick from the 57 pairings, matching this direction):
- Display:   a rounded medieval-adjacent serif or storybook display (NOT blackletter — readable and friendly)
- Body:      humanist sans-serif, high x-height, 16–18px base
- Accent:    a single italic or hand-drawn style for lie detector verdicts only
- Type scale: 1.250 (major third) modular scale
- Line height: 1.55 body, 1.15 display

SPACING & LAYOUT:
- Spacing scale (4px base): 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128
- Container max-width: 1280px
- Grid: 12-col desktop, 8-col tablet, 4-col mobile
- Section vertical rhythm: 96px desktop / 64px tablet / 48px mobile

RADII:
- sm 8px  |  md 16px  |  lg 24px  |  xl 32px  |  pill 9999px
Default card radius: lg. Buttons: pill. Lie detector meter housing: xl.

ELEVATION (soft claymorphism — no hard drop shadows):
- sm  0 2px 8px   rgba(espresso / 0.08)
- md  0 8px 24px  rgba(espresso / 0.10)
- lg  0 16px 48px rgba(espresso / 0.12)

MOTION LANGUAGE:
- Easing:     cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) for entrances (soft over-settle)
- Durations:  micro 150ms, standard 300ms, expressive 600ms, hero 900ms
- Stagger:    80ms between siblings on scroll-reveal
- Hover:      CTA lift 2px + shadow md → lg, 200ms
- Hero:       title fade+rise 24px over 900ms, 120ms per-word stagger
- Lie Detector: needle swing 600ms with overshoot ease, LED blink 150ms
- prefers-reduced-motion: all transform/opacity transitions collapse to opacity only at 200ms

STEP 5 — SECTIONS & LAYOUT (for Stitch)
==============================================================
Section order and goal:

9. FAQ             — accordion (Is this legally binding? Can I use these for real? etc.)
10. Footer         — fine print ("For entertainment purposes only. We are not responsible for jousting-related absences.")

Per-image prompts:

- Lie Detector    — close-up of fantastical wooden dashboard with gauge needle pointing to "Plausible," warm amber and red glowing LEDs, carved oak housing with rounded corners, Pixar 3D style
- Category Icons  — 5 small rounded 3D icons on cream parchment: rolled scroll (Quest), crossed wooden swords (Jousting), cracked heart locket (Courtly Love), cute green dragon head (Dragon), quill writing on blank shield (Custom)
- Excuse Output   — close-up of a worn parchment scroll with handwritten medieval-style excuse text, wax seal with paw print, feathered quill resting nearby, candlelit warm glow
- Knights cards   — 18 portrait renders, one per character from STEP 1, each in a different dog breed, wearing character-appropriate Camelot attire, consistent 3:4 framing and palette

Knight-card consistency rules (all 18 must share):
- Framing:    3:4 portrait, chest-up, subject centered, 10% breathing room at top and bottom
- Lighting:   same golden-hour key light from camera-left at ~30°, soft warm fill from camera-right, subtle rim light on fur edges. No high-contrast or noir lighting on any single knight. Background blur matches across all 18
- Palette:    pulls from Step 3 tokens; character-specific accent allowed (Lancelot red, Galahad white-gold, Mordred deep purple, Gawain green) but never overrides the global warm palette
- Pose:       three-quarter view, eye-line to camera, friendly expression. Villains (Mordred, Agravain) may be sterner but never menacing — this is Pixar, not Grimdark
- Breed:      one unique breed per character as assigned in STEP 1. No breed repeats across the 18
- Attire:     character-appropriate Camelot (tabard, cape, circlet, mantle). Subtle wear/patina where fitting
- Naming:     files as `knight-<name>.png` (lowercase, hyphenated)
